    3 fixes for the next call

    1. Ask the motive question before explaining the demo."What made you click on our ad — what are you hoping this could change for you?"
    2. Answer "is it safe" with the licence and segregated funds, not with reassurance."We are licensed by the Seychelles FSA, licence SD044, and client money is kept separately."
    3. Close on a day and time, not "I'll call you later"."I'll call you Thursday at 19:00 to see how the demo feels. Does that work?"
