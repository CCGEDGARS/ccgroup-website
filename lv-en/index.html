Masterclass 13–4 hours

The language of influence

Sleight of Mouth · Robert Dilts + Milton Model · Milton Erickson

Milton Erickson’s soft language that lowers resistance — truisms, presuppositions, embedded commands, pacing and leading. Plus Robert Dilts’s 14 patterns for working with customer beliefs: “all lenders are crooks”, “I won’t be able to pay anyway”, “I don’t need this”. Persuasive — without pressure.

Masterclass 23–4 hours

Debt collection

From the first missed payment to a promise that is kept

Built on Edgars Untals’ own collection scripts: the reminder call before the due date, the first conversation about the debt with options A, B and C, answers to “I have no money”, a promise to pay with a specific amount and date, and the final call — firm, factual and within the law. We measure promises kept, not promises made.

Masterclass 33–4 hours

Advanced negotiation

Black Swan · Harvard

The full system of Chris Voss and the Harvard Negotiation Project, applied to your situations: debt restructuring, partial payments, terms for large and repeat customers, negotiations with partners. Anchors, BATNA and agreements that get kept.
